This Privacy Policy explains how Family Chores collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the mobile application on iOS and Android.

By using Family Chores, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Terms of Service

1) Information We Collect

A. Account and Profile Information

When you create or use an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials and sign-in metadata handled through secure authentication services such as Supabase, Apple, and Google
  • Display name
  • Role (parent or child)
  • Optional emoji/profile customizations
  • Authentication provider identifiers and session data needed to sign you in and keep you logged in

If you use Apple Sign In, we may receive your Apple account identifier and email relay address if provided by Apple.

If you use Google Sign In, we may receive your Google account identifier, email address, name, profile photo, and an ID token used to authenticate your session.

B. Family and App Content

To provide app functionality, we store information you create in the app, including:

  • Family details (family name, family code, parent relationship, optional parent PIN)
  • Child profiles (display name, emoji, status, points)
  • Chores (title, description, assignments, schedule/recurrence, points)
  • Chore completion records (date, status, approvals)
  • Rewards and reward claims (title, points required, claim/approval status)
  • Join requests and related status history
  • Point modification history

C. Subscription and Purchase Information

For subscription management, we use RevenueCat and app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play). We may process:

  • App user ID (to map subscription entitlements)
  • Entitlement/subscription status
  • Product and offering identifiers
  • Expiration and plan status metadata
  • Receipt and transaction metadata returned by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card information. Payments are processed by Apple and Google through in-app purchase systems.

D. Push Notification Data

If you allow notifications, we collect and store push notification tokens and notification permission status.

E. Device and Technical Data

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate the app, including device model, operating system version, app version, logs and error diagnostics, and basic network/session metadata used for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

F. Local Device Storage

We store limited app state on your device, such as active child mode/session state and push token caching, to support app features.

2) How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users and keep sessions active
  • Support Apple Sign In, Google Sign In, and password login
  • Enable family management and role-based access (parent/child)
  • Create, assign, and track chores and rewards
  • Provide points, approvals, and history features
  • Manage subscriptions and feature access based on entitlements
  • Send notifications when enabled
  • Maintain app security, reliability, and abuse prevention
  • Provide customer support and troubleshoot issues

3) Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Depending on your location, we process data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract
  • Legitimate interests
  • Consent
  • Legal obligations

4) How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information.

We share data only as needed with service providers that help operate the app, such as Supabase, Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and push notification infrastructure providers used by the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5) Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

Retention examples:

  • Account and core family/task records are retained while your account is active
  • Some historical records may be retained to preserve integrity of family history, logs, and audit trails
  • Some items may be soft-deactivated rather than immediately hard-deleted within app workflows
  • Subscription and receipt metadata may be retained as needed to verify entitlement history and prevent fraud

6) Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, restrict, or port your personal information where applicable.

In-app options currently include sign out, password reset, account deletion request flow, and subscription management in the App Store / Google Play subscription settings.

You may also control notifications in your iOS/Android system settings.

Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising child use and managing child profile information entered into the app. If you believe a child has provided data inappropriately, contact us so we can review and address it.

If you add a child profile or invite a child to a family, you represent that you have the authority to do so and that any information you provide about that child is submitted by or with the permission of a parent or legal guardian.
